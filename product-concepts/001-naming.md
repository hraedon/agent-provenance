# Naming shortlist

The working name `agent-provenance` is descriptive but uninspired. Candidates,
roughly grouped by mental model.

## Mental model: "trail of immutable markers"

- **cairn** — A stack of stones marking a path. Compact, memorable, evocative
  of the "trail of actions left behind" image. Easy to say, easy to type. No
  obvious naming collisions in the agent-tooling space.
- **stele** — An ancient inscribed monument; literal "immutable record carved
  in stone." Stronger image than cairn, but a less-familiar word.

## Mental model: "durable record"

- **vellum** — Parchment for durable inscription. Evocative, slightly literary.
  Domain question: vellum.com is a YC-backed contract-management company; not
  a hard collision but worth noting.
- **codex** — A bound book of records. Strong "audit log" connotation. Heavily
  overloaded in tech (OpenAI Codex, multiple other products) — probably ruled
  out.

## Mental model: "the official witness"

- **auspex** — A Roman official who interpreted omens / served as an
  authoritative witness. Strong "auditor / attestation" connotation. Slightly
  obscure but memorable once learned.
- **notarius** / **notary** — Direct but generic; "notary" is overloaded with
  blockchain projects.

## Mental model: "what gets handed to the auditor"

- **receipts** — Plural noun, modern internet-flavored. ("Show me the
  receipts.") Casual, but the casual register might be a feature rather
  than a bug — it signals the tool's job concretely. Risk: feels too cute
  for a procurement document.
- **provenant** — Invented (from *provenire*, the root of "provenance").
  Direct but coined-feeling.

## Mental model: derived from regista

- **stratum** — A layer of regista; literal continuation of the metaphor.
  Slightly heavy.
- **strata** — Plural. Same.

## Recommendation order (subjective, owner picks)

1. **cairn** — best balance of memorable, evocative, undiluted by other uses
2. **auspex** — strong audit/witness connotation, slightly more obscure
3. **stele** — beautiful image, weaker brand recognition
4. **vellum** — good if the YC collision doesn't bother you
5. **receipts** — strongest if you want the casual register; weakest if you
   want enterprise gravitas

If none of these stick, "agent-provenance" remains a perfectly acceptable
working name and we can rename later. The cost of renaming pre-v0.1 is near
zero; the cost of picking the wrong name and being stuck with it is real.
Better to keep the descriptive placeholder than commit early to a name that
doesn't sit right.
